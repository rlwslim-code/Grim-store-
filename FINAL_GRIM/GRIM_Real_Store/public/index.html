HOW CAN WE HELP?

Quick answers before you order and support when you need it.

How does GRIM preorder work?

Your order is recorded after checkout and receives an order number. Production and delivery updates can then be communicated using your contact details.

Can I change the size after ordering?

Contact customer care as soon as possible with your order number. Changes depend on whether production for that order has begun.

How do I get help with an order?

Use the Customer Care button and include your order number, email and the issue you need help with.
